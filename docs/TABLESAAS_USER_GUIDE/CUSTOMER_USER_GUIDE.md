# Customer Guide — Ordering with a TableSaaS QR Menu

No app and no account needed: everything works in your phone's browser.

## 1. Scan the QR code
Point your phone camera at the QR code on your table and tap the link. The restaurant's menu opens with your table already selected; you'll see **Table T4** (for example) at the top. If you see *"QR code expired"*, ask the staff for a fresh code (the restaurant may have replaced it).

You can also open the restaurant's website directly (e.g. `the-broast.yourdomain.com`) to order takeaway or browse.

## 2. Explore the menu
- The top of the page shows the restaurant, its opening hours, address, phone, order types and guest rating.
- **Today's offers** shows dishes on promotion; **Chef's picks** shows the restaurant's highlights.
- **Explore the menu** jumps to the full menu. The category bar stays at the top and follows you as you scroll; tap a category to jump to it.
- **Search dishes…** finds dishes by name, description or tag.
- 🌶 means spicy, 🍃 vegetarian. **Sold out** dishes can't be ordered.
- Use the language switcher at the top right to read the menu in English, Urdu or Arabic (when the restaurant offers them).

## 3. Choose a dish
Tap a dish to see its photos (swipe for more), description and price, then:
- **Choose an option** if it has sizes or variants (e.g. Half / Full);
- tick any **Extras**;
- add a note for the kitchen (e.g. "no onions");
- set the quantity with − / +;
- tap **Add to order**. A confirmation appears and the order bar at the bottom shows your item count and total.

## 4. Review and place the order
1. Tap **View order** at the bottom.
2. Change quantities, or remove items with the bin icon.
3. Choose **Dine-in** or **Takeaway** (whichever the restaurant offers).
4. Optionally add your name, phone number, notes, and a **coupon code** (tap **Apply**).
5. Check the summary (subtotal, discount, service charge, tax, total). The restaurant confirms the final amount.
6. Tap **Place order**.

## 5. Track your order
You're taken to a live tracking page: **Received → Confirmed → Being prepared → Ready! → Served**, updating by itself. Bookmark the page or keep it open; the link is private to your order.

## 6. Pay
- **Pay at the counter / to your waiter** (cash or card terminal), or
- **Pay online now**, shown only if the restaurant has set up online card payments. The order shows *Paid* only after the payment is confirmed by the card processor.

## 7. Afterwards
Once your order is served you can **rate your experience** (1–5 stars with an optional comment). The restaurant may publish it under *What guests say*.

## 8. Reserve a table
If the restaurant offers reservations, tap the calendar icon (**Book a table**), enter your name, phone, guests, date and time → **Submit**. The restaurant confirms your request.
