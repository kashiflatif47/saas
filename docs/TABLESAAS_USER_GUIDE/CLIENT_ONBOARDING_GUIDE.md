# Welcome to TableSaaS

*A short guide for restaurant owners. No technical knowledge needed.*

## What you get
- **Your own restaurant website** with your logo, colours and menu, at your own address (for example `yourname.yourplatform.com`).
- **QR ordering:** a QR code on every table. Guests scan it, see your menu on their phone and order. No app needed.
- **A live dashboard** where new orders appear instantly with a sound, for you, your kitchen and your waiters.
- **Kitchen screen, staff accounts, payments, reports**, and depending on your plan, stock control, reservations and loyalty points.

## Getting access
Your platform provider sets up your restaurant and sends you:
1. your **restaurant address**, and
2. your **sign-in email**, plus a **temporary password**, sent separately for safety.

## Step 1 — Sign in and secure your account
Open `your-address/login`, sign in, then click the **key icon** at the bottom left and set your own password.

## Step 2 — Make it yours (Branding)
Left menu → **Branding**: upload your **logo** and a **hero photo** (your best food or dining-room photo, landscape), choose your colours (or a ready-made preset), write a short headline and "About us" text → **Publish changes**. Your website keeps its professional layout; only your brand changes.

## Step 3 — Opening hours and contact details
**Settings → General**: your phone and address. **Settings → Ordering & tax**: opening hours, tax and service charge, and whether guests can order dine-in, takeaway or both → **Save**.

## Step 4 — Add your menu
**Menu → + Category** (e.g. Starters, BBQ, Drinks), then **+ Dish** for each item: name, price, description, and options such as *Half / Full* or extras. After saving a dish, add a photo; dishes with photos sell better. Mark highlights as **Featured**, and switch **Available** off when something runs out.

## Step 5 — Tables and QR codes
**Tables & QR → Add tables** (one at a time, or several at once) → **Print all (PDF)**. Cut the cards and place one on each table. Test each one with your phone camera.

## Step 6 — Add your team
**Staff & roles → Add staff**: give each person their own login and a role: **Manager**, **Waiter**, **Cashier**, **Kitchen** or **Inventory manager**. Each role only sees what it needs.

## Step 7 — Receive orders
Keep **Orders** open at the counter and the **Kitchen display** on a kitchen tablet. A new order makes a sound → **Accept** → the kitchen taps **Ready** → the waiter taps **Mark served** → collect payment (**Collect payment** → Cash or Card terminal) → **Complete & close**.

## How your guests use it
Scan the table code → browse → tap a dish → **Add to order** → **View order** → **Place order** → follow the live status on their phone → pay at the counter or to the waiter (or online, if you've set that up) → leave a rating, which you can publish on your website.

## Need help?
Contact your platform provider. They can reset passwords, unlock accounts and manage your subscription. More detail: *Restaurant Owner User Guide*.
